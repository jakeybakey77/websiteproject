Common questions

How the prototype handles inquiries and demo data.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

You’ll see a confirmation and a reference. Synthetic submissions are stored on the demo server for simulated review.

Is Greenwich Credit Partners a lender?

Greenwich Credit Partners is a demonstration prototype. It does not operate as a lender or financing brokerage.

Will this affect my credit?

The demo runs no credit check and asks for no banking credentials or uploaded financial documents.

What information should I use?

Use fictional business information. The demo provides a fictional contact profile.
