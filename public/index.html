COMMON QUESTIONS

What should I know?

A transparent explanation of what this early demonstration can and cannot do.

Is Greenwich Credit Partners a lender?

No. This is a demonstration of a potential merchant inquiry experience, not an operating lender or commercial-financing brokerage. The website cannot approve, decline, arrange, or fund financing.

Will using the form affect my credit?

No. The demonstration runs no credit check and does not ask for a Social Security number, banking credentials, or uploaded financial documents.

What happens after I complete the inquiry?

You'll see a simulated confirmation. No information is sent to a funding partner or stored on a server. A future live system would require reviewed disclosures, permissions, and a secure processing workflow.

What personal details should I enter?

Fictional business information only. The contact profile is generated for the demo; do not submit real customer or financial information.

Are specific financing products or terms available?

Not through this prototype. The examples on the homepage describe potential uses of capital, not offers, eligibility requirements, lending terms, or lender relationships.
